Privacy Policy

GRJ Office
Effective date: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GRJ (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when employees and authorized contractors use the GRJ Office mobile application (“App”). The App is an organization-facing tool for attendance and office operations; it is not intended for children.

Information we handle

  • Account and employment information: login credentials or authentication tokens, name, employee or contractor identifiers, email address, phone number, role, assigned work site, and employment or payroll-related details made available through the organization.
  • Precise location: GPS coordinates and location accuracy when you punch in or punch out. The App also accesses location in the background while an attendance session is punched in, to support work-location attendance. On Android, the operating system may show an ongoing location notification. Background tracking is stopped when you punch out. The App keeps the latest coordinates locally on your device and sends punch-in/punch-out coordinates to our service with the attendance record. The code does not send each background tracking update to our service.
  • Identity and work records: information and documents you or your organization provide for employee verification (which may include government ID details, bank details, and profile photographs), attendance, leave, tasks, chat messages, purchases, expenses, inventory, and reports.
  • Images and documents: photos or files you choose to capture, select, upload, or download for work functions such as purchases, records, or verification. Camera access is used when you choose an in-app capture function. The App does not need broad photo-library access to access images selected through the system picker.
  • Device and notification information: notification permission and a Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to deliver work notifications. Basic technical and diagnostic information may be processed by the operating system or service providers to operate and secure the App.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate authorized users; operate attendance and work-site checks; manage employee and contractor records; support verification, payroll, leave, tasks, messaging, purchases, expenses, inventory, and reports; deliver notifications; maintain security; troubleshoot the App; and meet legal or organizational obligations.

When location is accessed

Foreground location is used to verify that a punch-in occurs within the configured work-site radius and to record the location associated with punch-in or punch-out. If you remain punched in, the App can continue to access location in the background for work attendance tracking until you punch out. Location is not used to serve advertising or to build advertising profiles. You can control location permissions in your device settings; disabling access may prevent location-based attendance functions from working.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information is made available to authorized GRJ personnel and organization administrators who need it to manage work. The App sends information to the GRJ Office service hosted at admin.tripnic.io to provide its features. We may use service providers that process information for hosting, authentication, storage, or push notifications (including Firebase/Google services). These providers may process information only to provide or support their services to us. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business reorganization.

Storage, security, and retention

Information is transmitted to our service over HTTPS and is stored locally where needed for App operation (for example, the latest location coordinates and session information). We and our service providers use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Work and attendance records are retained for as long as needed for the purposes described above, the organization’s relationship with the user, and applicable legal or accounting requirements; they are then deleted or de-identified under the organization’s retention practices. Some records may remain in backups for a limited period.

Your choices and requests

You can manage camera, location, and notification permissions in your device settings. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your work account, contact your GRJ organization administrator or email tdhoffice824@gmail.com. We may need to retain certain records where required by law or legitimate employment, security, or accounting needs. Removing the App does not by itself delete information already held by the organization or its service providers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or our practices change. We will post the updated policy at this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies, subject to any consent required by law.

Contact
